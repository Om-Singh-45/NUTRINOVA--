WEIGHT LOSS NUTRITION GUIDE

Safe and sustainable weight loss occurs through a combination of proper nutrition, portion control, and healthy lifestyle habits.

FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES:

1. CALORIC DEFICIT
   - Create a deficit of 500-750 calories per day
   - This leads to 0.5-1 kg weight loss per week
   - Never go below 1,200 calories/day (women) or 1,500 calories/day (men)
   - Extreme restriction can slow metabolism

2. PROTEIN INTAKE
   - Essential for preserving muscle mass during weight loss
   - Aim for 1.6-2.2g per kg of body weight
   - Include protein in every meal
   - Sources: chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, legumes, tofu

3. FIBER CONSUMPTION
   - Increases satiety and reduces hunger
   - Target 25-35g per day
   - Sources: vegetables, fruits, whole grains, legumes
   - Helps regulate blood sugar and digestion

BEST FOODS FOR WEIGHT LOSS:

1. LEAN PROTEINS
   - Chicken breast (skinless)
   - Turkey
   - Fish (salmon, cod, tuna)
   - Eggs and egg whites
   - Low-fat Greek yogurt
   - Cottage cheese
   - Tofu and tempeh
   - Legumes (beans, lentils)

2. VEGETABLES (LOW CALORIE, HIGH VOLUME)
   - Leafy greens (spinach, kale, lettuce)
   - Broccoli and cauliflower
   - Zucchini and cucumber
   - Bell peppers
   - Tomatoes
   - Mushrooms
   - Asparagus

3. WHOLE GRAINS (MODERATE PORTIONS)
   - Quinoa (1/2 cup cooked)
   - Brown rice (1/2 cup cooked)
   - Oats (1/2 cup dry)
   - Whole wheat bread (1-2 slices)

4. HEALTHY FATS (SMALL PORTIONS)
   - Avocado (1/4 to 1/2)
   - Nuts (small handful, 20-30g)
   - Seeds (chia, flax, pumpkin)
   - Olive oil (1-2 tablespoons)
   - Fatty fish (salmon, mackerel)

5. FRUITS (MODERATE AMOUNTS)
   - Berries (strawberries, blueberries)
   - Apples and pears
   - Citrus fruits (oranges, grapefruit)
   - Watermelon and cantaloupe
   - Limit to 2-3 servings per day

FOODS TO AVOID OR MINIMIZE:

1. HIGH-CALORIE, LOW-NUTRIENT FOODS
   - Sugary beverages (soda, juice, energy drinks)
   - Candy and sweets
   - Chips and processed snacks
   - Fast food and fried foods
   - Pastries and baked goods
   - Ice cream and desserts

2. REFINED CARBOHYDRATES
   - White bread and pasta
   - White rice
   - Sugary cereals
   - Crackers and cookies

3. EXCESSIVE FATS
   - Deep-fried foods
   - Fatty cuts of meat
   - Full-fat dairy products
   - Creamy sauces and dressings
   - Excessive butter or oil

MEAL STRUCTURE FOR WEIGHT LOSS:

BREAKFAST (300-400 calories)
- Protein source + whole grain + fruit/vegetable
- Example: 2 eggs, 1 slice whole wheat toast, berries

LUNCH (400-500 calories)
- Large portion vegetables + lean protein + small portion carbs
- Example: Grilled chicken salad with quinoa

DINNER (400-500 calories)
- Vegetables (50% of plate) + protein (25%) + carbs (25%)
- Example: Salmon, steamed broccoli, sweet potato

SNACKS (100-150 calories each, 1-2 per day)
- Greek yogurt with berries
- Apple with almond butter
- Vegetables with hummus
- Handful of nuts

HYDRATION FOR WEIGHT LOSS:

- Drink 8-10 glasses of water daily
- Drink 1-2 glasses before each meal (increases satiety)
- Green tea can boost metabolism slightly
- Avoid all sugary drinks
- Limit alcohol (empty calories)

EATING HABITS:

1. PORTION CONTROL
   - Use smaller plates (9-10 inches)
   - Measure food initially to learn portions
   - Eat slowly and chew thoroughly
   - Stop eating when 80% full

2. MEAL TIMING
   - Don't skip breakfast
   - Eat every 3-4 hours
   - Avoid eating 2-3 hours before bed
   - Consider intermittent fasting (16:8) if suitable

3. MINDFUL EATING
   - Remove distractions (TV, phone)
   - Focus on your food
   - Identify true hunger vs. emotional eating
   - Keep a food diary

EXERCISE COMBINATION:

- Cardio: 150-300 minutes per week (moderate intensity)
- Strength training: 2-3 times per week
- Daily movement: Walk 8,000-10,000 steps

REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS:

- Healthy weight loss: 0.5-1 kg per week
- First 2 weeks may show more loss (water weight)
- Progress may slow after initial period
- Focus on non-scale victories (energy, mood, fitness)
- Expect plateaus - adjust calories or exercise as needed

TIPS TO STAY CONSISTENT:

- Meal prep on weekends
- Keep healthy snacks ready
- Plan meals in advance
- Allow occasional treats (80/20 rule)
- Track progress with photos and measurements
- Get adequate sleep (7-9 hours)
- Manage stress (meditation, yoga)
- Find accountability (friend, group, app)

RED FLAGS - WHAT NOT TO DO:

- Don't eliminate entire food groups
- Avoid crash diets or detoxes
- Don't rely on supplements alone
- Avoid extremely low-calorie diets
- Don't ignore hunger signals completely
- Avoid comparing your journey to others

Remember: Sustainable weight loss is a gradual process. Focus on building healthy habits rather than quick fixes.
